\section*{Tuples and the Cartesian Product}
\textbf{Tuple}: ordered sequence of objects, objects may occur multiple times. $(o_1, o_2, ... o_k)$ or $\langle o_1, o_2, ... o_k\rangle$ \\
The \textbf{Cartesian Product} $S_1 \times  ... \times S_n$ of sets $S_1, ... S_n$ is the following set of n-tuples: $S_1 \times ... \times S_n = \{\langle x_1, ..., x_n\rangle\ \mid x_1 \in S_1, x_2 \in S_2, ... x_n \in S_n\}$
\vspace{0.5cm} \\
The \textbf{k-ary Catesian Power} of a set S (with $k \in \mathbb{N}_1$) is the set $S^k = \{\langle o_1, ..., o_k\rangle \mid o_i \in S \text{ for all } i \in \{1, ..., k\}\} = S \times ... \times S$ (k times)
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=0pt,leftmargin=*]
    \item The cartesian product is not commutative ($A \times B \neq B \times A$) and not associative 
    \item $A \times \emptyset = \emptyset \times A = \emptyset$
\end{itemize}
